REAL BRITAIN, AS DOOM LEVELSpostcode2wad

— of Britain rebuilt as playable Doom, so far.

Every square on the map is 800 m of real England, generated from Environment Agency 1 m LIDAR and OpenStreetMap: real building footprints at measured heights, sloped terrain, hedges, trees. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, click a square to walk it. WASD moves and the mouse looks on a desktop; phones get on-screen controls.

Work in progress. All 13 districts of Kent are built — other counties follow as their LIDAR is mirrored. Expect rough edges: this is a project in the open, not a finished product.
