REAL BRITAIN, AS DOOM LEVELSpostcode2wad

Every square below is 800 m of real England, generated from Environment Agency 1 m LIDAR and OpenStreetMap: real building footprints at measured heights, sloped terrain, hedges, trees. Click a square to walk it. WASD moves and the mouse looks on a desktop; phones get on-screen controls. Dark squares are the sea.

Work in progress. Thanet is the only district built so far — more regions are coming as the LIDAR for them is mirrored. Expect rough edges: this is a project in the open, not a finished product.
